I'm Abhinav, a student at Independence High School in Virginia. I compete in CTFs, train through SANS, and study digital forensics to understand how systems break and how to defend them.

Outside security, I build games in Java and Unreal Engine 5. I like turning an idea into something I can test, play, and keep improving.
