Bring curious people together.

I co-founded IndyHAX, a hybrid capture-the-flag competition and hackathon open to high school students.

The idea is to bring students together to solve cybersecurity challenges and build hands-on coding projects. It connects the two things I keep coming back to: figuring out how technology works and learning alongside other people.
